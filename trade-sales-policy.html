Trade & Export Sale Policy

AnyBike supplies used motorcycles on a trade/export basis. This page explains the standard commercial basis we intend to apply to purchases from AnyBike. A specific Formal Offer, invoice or written agreement may contain additional transaction-specific terms.

Core sale basis
Trade / business purchase
Used motorcycles
No automatic AnyBike mechanical warranty
Mechanical checks by independent third party when requested

1. Trade buyer status

Motorcycles purchased from AnyBike are intended for buyers acting wholly or mainly for purposes relating to their trade, business, craft or profession. Before a purchase is completed, AnyBike may require confirmation of the buyer's trade/business status and intended use.

2. Used motorcycle basis

Motorcycles are used vehicles. Age, mileage, prior use and disclosed condition are relevant to the transaction. AnyBike records the description and known information available to it and does not represent a used motorcycle as new or mechanically fault-free.

3. No automatic mechanical warranty

Unless a Formal Offer expressly states otherwise, AnyBike does not include a separate voluntary mechanical warranty or guarantee with a trade/export motorcycle sale. This does not exclude any liability which cannot lawfully be excluded.

4. Collection condition check

AnyBike or its collection partner may record the motorcycle's physical appearance and handover condition, including photographs, visible damage, mileage, keys, documents and identity details where accessible.

  • This is a visual/handover check.
  • It is not a mechanical or engineering inspection.
  • It does not verify hidden defects or future reliability.

5. Mechanical inspection

A buyer who requires a mechanical assessment must request an independent third-party inspection before purchase or before the transaction reaches the agreed commitment point. The inspection is carried out by an appropriately qualified external provider and the cost is payable by the buyer unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing.

6. History and documents

AnyBike may obtain or display vehicle-history information, MOT data, service information, seller statements and available documents. These are recorded as information sources, not as a mechanical guarantee. Where information is not verified, it should be treated as unverified.

7. Buyer inspection choice

The transaction may record one of the following inspection bases:

  • Visual Collection Check Only
  • Independent Mechanical Inspection Requested
  • Independent Mechanical Inspection Completed
  • Buyer Arranged Own Inspection
  • Buyer Declined Additional Inspection

8. Export and import responsibilities

Destination-country import eligibility, duties, taxes, registration, compliance and local use requirements vary. The buyer is responsible for ensuring the motorcycle can be lawfully imported, registered and used in the destination country unless AnyBike expressly agrees to provide a specific service.

9. Shipping and handover

The Formal Offer should identify the agreed UK handover point, delivery arrangement or freight provider. Risk, responsibility and shipping obligations are governed by the terms agreed for that transaction and should not be assumed merely from the fact that the motorcycle is being exported.

10. Accurate description and known issues

AnyBike's trade/export model does not permit known material problems to be concealed. Known defects, discrepancies or relevant limitations identified before sale should be disclosed through the motorcycle information, inspection record, Formal Offer or other transaction documents as appropriate.

Important legal note. This page is a commercial policy summary, not a substitute for the specific contract for an individual motorcycle. AnyBike's final trade sale terms should be read together with the Formal Offer, invoice, inspection choice and handover terms. Nothing on this page is intended to exclude rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.
