About AnyBike

A different way to buy and trade UK motorcycles.

AnyBike brings together two motorcycle buying routes: used UK motorcycles for genuine trade and business buyers worldwide, and selected new VMoto electric motorcycles and scooters for UK retail customers.

On our used-bike export route, AnyBike sources, purchases and resells the motorcycle to the trade buyer. On our VMoto UK retail route, customers can choose a new motorcycle, place an order through AnyBike and have it prepared and delivered through the UK retail journey.

How AnyBike works

AnyBike is the buyer and the seller.

Our model is deliberately different from a classified website. The supplying dealer or seller remains confidential to the buyer, AnyBike handles the commercial transaction, and the buyer purchases from AnyBike.

1. UK motorcycles enter the AnyBike supply network Authorised dealer feeds, direct dealer supply, private sellers and other approved UK sources provide motorcycles for AnyBike to assess, source and purchase.
2. AnyBike understands buyer demand International buyers and motorcycle trade customers tell us what they need, or browse motorcycles already available through AnyBike.
3. AnyBike agrees the commercial terms Where a motorcycle is suitable, AnyBike confirms availability, purchase terms, costs and the buyer proposition.
4. AnyBike buys and resells the motorcycle AnyBike purchases the motorcycle from the supplier and sells it onward to the qualifying buyer.
Experience

Approximately 40 years of motorcycle industry experience behind AnyBike.

The experience behind AnyBike spans approximately 40 years across motorcycle retail, distribution and transport. That practical industry background shapes how AnyBike approaches stock, dealers, buyers, collection, logistics and international transactions.

Why AnyBike exists

Motorcycles should move more intelligently between markets.

The UK has a large and diverse motorcycle market, while demand varies significantly by country and by buyer type. AnyBike uses that market knowledge to identify commercially suitable motorcycles and help move the right stock to the right buyers.
