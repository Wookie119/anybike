About AnyBike

A different way to buy and trade UK motorcycles.

AnyBike is a motorcycle trading platform built to bring authorised UK motorcycle supply together with international retail buyers, international trade buyers and UK motorcycle trade customers.

Unlike a traditional classified marketplace, AnyBike does not simply introduce buyers to supplying dealers. Where a transaction proceeds, AnyBike agrees the purchase, buys the motorcycle and resells it to the customer.

How AnyBike works

AnyBike is the buyer and the seller.

Our model is deliberately different from a classified website. The supplying dealer or seller remains confidential to the buyer, AnyBike handles the commercial transaction, and the buyer purchases from AnyBike.

1. UK motorcycles enter the AnyBike supply network Authorised dealer feeds, direct dealer supply, private sellers and other approved UK sources provide motorcycles for AnyBike to assess, source and purchase.
2. AnyBike understands buyer demand International buyers and motorcycle trade customers tell us what they need, or browse motorcycles already available through AnyBike.
3. AnyBike agrees the commercial terms Where a motorcycle is suitable, AnyBike confirms availability, purchase terms, costs and the buyer proposition.
4. AnyBike buys and resells the motorcycle AnyBike purchases the motorcycle from the supplier and sells it onward to the qualifying buyer.
Experience

Approximately 40 years of motorcycle industry experience behind AnyBike.

The experience behind AnyBike spans approximately 40 years across motorcycle retail, distribution and transport. That practical industry background shapes how AnyBike approaches stock, dealers, buyers, collection, logistics and international transactions.

Why we are building AnyBike

Motorcycles should move more intelligently between markets.

The UK has a large and diverse motorcycle market, while demand varies significantly by country and by buyer type. AnyBike is being built to understand that demand, identify commercially suitable motorcycles and move the right stock to the right market.

Who AnyBike sells to

International buyers and motorcycle trade customers.

AnyBike has a clear market boundary. International buyers can purchase privately or as a trade business. Within the UK, motorcycle sales through AnyBike are trade-only.

🌍

International Retail

Private buyers outside the United Kingdom can buy suitable motorcycles from AnyBike for international delivery or export.

🏢

International Trade

Motorcycle dealers, traders, importers and professional buyers outside the UK can source individual bikes or regular stock through AnyBike.

🤝

UK Trade

Verified UK motorcycle dealers and traders can buy motorcycles through AnyBike, including dealer-to-dealer trading opportunities.

⛔

No UK Retail Sales

AnyBike does not sell motorcycles to the general public in the UK. UK motorcycle sales through AnyBike are for motorcycle trade buyers only.
