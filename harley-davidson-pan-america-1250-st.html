Harley-Davidson Pan America 1250 ST

The road-focused member of the Pan America family, launched for model year 2025 with 17-inch road wheels, lower suspension, Michelin Scorcher Sport tyres and the liquid-cooled Revolution Max 1250 engine.

The ST is a distinct model and specification from the Pan America 1250 Special. Check the exact model year for equipment and market differences.

Why Harley-Davidson introduced the ST

The Pan America platform arrived as Harley-Davidson's adventure motorcycle programme. The ST, introduced for 2025, turns that versatile formula towards fast road riding and sport touring, with a lower stance, road-biased chassis settings, reduced bodywork and 17-inch wheels.

It should be compared with road-focused touring and adventure-sport machines, not treated as an off-road version of the Special.

2025 launch and 2026 development

2025 introduced the Pan America 1250 ST with a Screamin' Eagle quickshifter, SHOWA suspension, Brembo brakes and road tyres. Harley-Davidson's 2026 UK publication describes clutchless quickshifting, revised rider modes and Adaptive Ride Height. Pan America 1250 ST versus Pan America 1250 Special

1250 ST: road-focused 17-inch wheels and tyres, lower suspension and sport-touring ergonomics.

1250 Special: adventure-focused specification and wheel/suspension package, more suitable for mixed-surface touring depending on tyres and equipment.
