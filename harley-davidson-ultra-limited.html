UK buyer's model-specific checklist

  • Verify Ultra Limited trim, year and engine variant.
  • Test heated grips, infotainment, passenger equipment and touring electrics where fitted.
  • Inspect Tour-Pak, panniers, keys, locks and luggage mounting points.

These are suggested inspection points rather than a substitute for a qualified mechanical inspection, HPI checks or verifying export documents.

Detailed model generations and factory references

Different year-and-trim versions can have different engine families, chassis and touring equipment. These two documented reference points help identify the correct motorcycle before requesting UK supply.

2015 Ultra Limited Low

Harley-Davidson announced the Ultra Limited Low for model year 2015, with a factory seat height of 25.6 inches (about 650 mm), ergonomics for shorter riders and Project RUSHMORE Touring features. This figure is for the Low variant only.
