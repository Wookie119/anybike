Retail journey.

The VMoto route will reuse AnyBike's messaging, notifications and Deal 360 foundations, but with retail-specific steps.

01Choose modelCustomer starts from the VMoto model page.
02Choose version & colourBattery/specification and colour are selected.
03Confirm stockManual stock feed first, API-ready for live availability.
04Order & prepareAnyBike secures the retail order and VMoto prepares/PDI's the sold unit.
05Home deliveryMove Motorcycles collects and delivers to the retail customer.
Launch control: the public model catalogue can be built before live checkout. Retail ordering should only be switched fully live once pricing, warranty/aftersales, B2C servicing and customer terms are confirmed.
