## Non-negotiable model distinctions
- The revised 2022 K 1600 B and Grand America share the EU5 160hp
  and 180Nm six-cylinder, but **Audio System 2.0 is optional on B
  and standard on Grand America**.
- The 2019 R 1250 RS 1,254cc ShiftCam 136hp model is not R 1250 R
  or R 1250 GS Adventure. Factory standard vs optional electronics
  remain separate in the stored JSON.
- The 2017 R nineT Urban G/S is a modern 1,170cc 110hp boxer, **not**
  the 1980 R 80 G/S. Urban G/S standard cast wheels and optional
  spoked wheels are recorded distinctly.
- No photos or stock feeds were copied without approval; no alternative
  templates, new layouts or hardcoded individual model pages were made.
- All 16 records were verified by the exact-model public Supabase RPC.
  Browser test cases were added to the existing live regression suite.
