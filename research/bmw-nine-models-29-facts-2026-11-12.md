## Safeguards
- The original F 800 ST and K 1200 GT 2005 HTML body has limited detailed data, so their first published records contain only the manufacturer announcement and positioning; no copied R1200S engine figures.
- R 1200 S launch ABS was **optional**, with 213kg full-tank weight, not dry weight.
- R nineT 2017 roadster has a 17-inch front wheel and USD fork; R nineT Urban G/S has a 19-inch front and conventional fork.
- K 1600 GT 2022 audio is **optional**, K 1600 GTL audio is **standard**.
- K 1300 R 173hp / 243kg is not K 1300 S output; R model has 180 rear tyre vs S model 190.
- R 1200 R 2015 liquid-cooled 125hp boxer is not the earlier air/oil-cooled generation. DTC and Dynamic ESA are optional, not standard.
- Manufacturer publication date is not first production date. All research facts have source URLs and exact model/year audit notes.
- No new photography without licensing, no model template or R1300GS changes, no manual 123-reg uploads.
