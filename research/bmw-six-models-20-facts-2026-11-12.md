## Exact-identity safeguards
- F 650 GS **Dakar single-cylinder** and **2008 parallel twin** are separate generations. 2008 F 650 GS does not have a 650cc engine just because of its model name.
- K 1200 S Italian 2004 launch preparation does not establish 2004 first customer deliveries or exact engine figures.
- R 1200 RS 2015 1170cc/125hp is not R 1250 RS 2019 1254cc/136hp.
- R 1200 RT 2014 liquid-cooled is not R 1250 RT 2019 ShiftCam.
- Factory optional Dynamic ESA, Riding Modes Pro, DTC and Hill Start Control are never labelled universal standard.
- No changes to approved model template, BMW R 1300 GS page, image licensing or UK/international research visibility.
