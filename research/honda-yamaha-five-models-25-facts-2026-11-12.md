## Exact-model safeguards
- Honda NX500 2024 is the renamed successor to CB500X, 471cc / 35kW, **not** a new 500cc engine.
- Honda CB650R is the naked model and CBR650R is the sports model. Both have optional E-Clutch for 2024; neither has E-Clutch universally standard. WMTC CO2 figures differ (CB650R 113g/km, CBR650R 112g/km).
- Yamaha MT-09 2024 standard is 193kg wet, **not** 196kg Y-AMT; the 119 rating is **PS**, not mechanically identical to 119 bhp.
- Yamaha XSR900 is the standard roadster, **not** the XSR900 GP.
- Factory WMTC fuel consumption is a test-cycle figure, not a real-world guarantee.
