## Editorial protections

- The F 700 GS uses a **798 cc** engine, not a 700 cc engine.
- F 700 GS and F 800 GS records apply to the **2013 model-year update**.
- F 800 GT launch equipment distinguishes standard ABS from optional ASC,
  ESA and luggage.
- F 800 R research concerns the **2014 announcement / updated model**,
  with specifications scoped to the following model year, not the first
  generation.
- BMW press-release dates are not represented as factory production dates.
- No manufacturer photo was imported without licensing metadata.
- Each record has a matching verification-audit entry in Supabase and is
  served through the existing exact-model public research RPC.
- The approved BMW R 1300 GS design and shared model template were not edited.
