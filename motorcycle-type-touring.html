About this motorcycle type

Touring motorcycles prioritise comfort on longer journeys, passenger accommodation, wind protection and luggage. They range from purpose-built luxury tourers to lighter sport-tourers.

What to consider before choosing

Comfort

Try the riding position, screen and weather protection over a realistic distance.

Weight

Fully loaded weight and low-speed handling can matter more than quoted power.

Luggage

Check factory cases, payload limits and passenger accommodation.
