Important Harley-Davidson model-year changes

Use these documented milestones to narrow down a UK sourcing enquiry. They describe particular model introductions or updates, not every motorcycle carrying the same name.

1965 · Electra Glide

Electric starting arrived on the FLH Electra Glide in 1965, a major milestone for Harley-Davidson touring motorcycles.

Harley-Davidson touring history ↗
1994 · Road King

The FLHR Road King joined the Touring range in 1994, drawing on earlier Electra Glide styling. Its heritage should not be confused with Softail-family cruisers.

Road King timeline ↗
2006 · Street Glide

The FLHX Street Glide joined the Touring family for model year 2006, adopting a lower-profile, custom-influenced appearance.

Street Glide introduction ↗
2009 · Touring chassis revision

The Touring range received a substantially revised chassis for model year 2009. Buyers comparing older motorcycles should check chassis generation as well as engine and trim.

2018 · New Softail platform

Harley-Davidson introduced a redesigned Softail family including Fat Boy, Breakout, Street Bob and Heritage Classic, with a new chassis, monoshock rear suspension and Milwaukee-Eight powertrain.

2025 · Street Bob update

For model year 2025, Harley-Davidson documented the Street Bob with a Milwaukee-Eight 117 Classic engine and updated equipment. Specifications vary by market, so confirm the exact UK motorcycle.

Harley-Davidson model history and engine generations

Harley-Davidson model names often span multiple generations. The engine, frame, electronic equipment and luggage specification must be checked against the exact motorcycle and its model year, rather than assumed from the model name.

Sportster and Iron 883

The original XL Sportster appeared in 1957. The Iron 883 joined the Dark Custom range in 2010; it belongs to the traditional air-cooled Sportster family, unlike the later liquid-cooled Sportster S.

Harley-Davidson Sportster history ↗

Sportster S and Nightster

The Revolution Max-powered Sportster S was introduced in 2021, with the liquid-cooled Nightster following in 2022. These newer models are distinct from the earlier Iron 883, and the engine displacement varies across the family.

Official Sportster timeline ↗

Fat Boy

The Fat Boy was introduced in 1990 on Harley-Davidson's Softail platform. Its characteristic solid-disc-style wheels helped establish the model's distinctive appearance. Engine size and running gear evolved in subsequent generations.

Harley-Davidson Fat Boy history ↗

Pan America 1250

Pan America models use the liquid-cooled Revolution Max V-twin, a different design from traditional air-cooled Harley-Davidson cruisers. Manufacturer service information specifies a 1,252 cc engine for documented 1250 variants; seat height, weight and equipment vary by version.

Harley-Davidson technical reference ↗

Road Glide and Street Glide

These are separate Grand American Touring models with different fairing configurations. The UK manufacturer brochure documents Milwaukee-Eight 117 engines for specific recent versions; that specification should not be applied to every earlier model year.

Harley-Davidson heritage and model generations

Founded in Milwaukee in 1903, Harley-Davidson's history spans traditional American V-twin cruisers, long-distance touring motorcycles, evolving Sportster and Softail families, and modern Revolution Max models. Names such as Road Glide and Electra Glide cover multiple generations whose engines, chassis and equipment are not interchangeable.

AnyBike will document verified model-year ranges, engine families and variants on individual guides as research is completed. Always confirm a motorcycle's VIN, registration history and exact specification before making an offer.
