Pan America 1250: adventure equipment and year differences

Pan America 1250 and 1250 Special specifications differ, and equipment may vary by year and market. Check the precise motorcycle rather than assuming all versions have adaptive ride height or the same wheels and suspension.

  • Chassis and protection: inspect wheel rims, tyre choice, underside protection and damage from off-road use where applicable.
  • Suspension: identify the fitted type and confirm any adaptive ride-height function only when installed.
  • Electronics: test ride modes, instruments, fault indicators, key operation and relevant documented software or recall work.
  • Travel accessories: check pannier mounts, luggage, handguards, crash bars and accessory wiring for sound installation.
