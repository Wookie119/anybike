# AnyBike country-page motorcycle manufacturer heritage source ledger

10 November 2026. These references document **manufacturer origins or historical association** for the shared country-page regional history layer. They do not certify current ownership, factories, motorcycles in stock, country of assembly, exports or imports.

One shared script `market-global-editorial-links.js` renders manufacturer context only for historically supported groups of countries. Every link opens the **top** of its existing manufacturer page, not `#models`. Do not copy this content into 217 separate hardcoded country HTML documents.
