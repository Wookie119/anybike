## Publication acceptance criteria

- Exact manufacturer/model identity and catalogue model ID confirmed; pending name reviews remain unpublished.
- Each fact has a supported category, primary or reliable source URL and verification audit.
- Model years, edition, market and generation are explicit where a spec varies.
- Public RPC and coverage report counts agree; approved images are distinct from images merely discovered.
- No new alternative page template or standalone dark research panel introduced.
- International exact-model stock is placed above research where stock is available.
- GitHub commit does not itself prove live deployment or browser rendering.
