# AnyBike — Underwrite, valuation, transport and CHIPS delivery plan
Updated: 12 October 2026. Status: approved design backlog; features below are NOT automatically implemented by this plan.
Repository: Wookie119/anybike (main). Supabase project: tuehtnezhdnkqbbhttgp.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Private seller, dealer and admin data separated by authenticated ownership, RLS / admin-only endpoints. No dealer identities, confidential bid amounts, AnyBike margin, Move quotation, contributions, or driver incentives shown on seller / buyer pages. No public-page notes explaining internal economics.
- Live submissions, bid histories, price histories, login, Available Stock OFF toggle must remain intact. No Vercel.
- A trade bid is NOT an AnyBike seller offer; no auto acceptance or sale. Seller must explicitly accept a formal offer and any subsequent collection price amendment.
- Move sets transport quotation; AnyBike administers underwriting economics. Internal cost is the Move quote, not an inferred regional tariff. Store estimated/quoted/confirmed status, origin/destination postcodes, quote version, timestamp, expiration, VAT treatment, and quote-change invalidation.
- AnyBike and Move operate within Giffords Empire Ltd today; preserve modular boundaries so either can later operate independently. No Move-to-AnyBike invoice assumed.

## Phased build
### 1. Admin reserve and margin integrity (first)
- Per-opportunity live commercial preview from highest valid active dealer bid, latest seller-stated minimum, Move quoted collection/delivery cost, other costs, minimum and target margin; proposed vs *agreed* seller/dealer contributions.
- Calculate net commercial value per bidder using bidder-specific delivery destination; highest gross bid may not win. Quote supplied by Move, not automatically generated by AnyBike; initially keyed by Admin, eventual Move API.
- Server-side require fresh confirmed Move quote and verified contributions before 'reserve_met' is written; use signed review revisions/quote identifiers. Invalidate approval upon newer seller price update, changes to active bids (including changed winner), transport quotation, other costs or contribution terms, and relevant checks. Do not use client checkbox as security control.
- Replace 'Seller current minimum confirmed' checkbox: seller-entered latest minimum is authoritative *asking position*, not a sale agreement. Maintain seller price-change audit.
- Keep HPI / finance check, seller vehicle availability, dealer bid validity, condition history, and final approval separate from preliminary reserve viability; final AnyBike formal offer gated.
- Road King AB-S-000025 / AB-UW-000015: minimum £5,000, active live bid £700; cannot be reserve met on that bid. Preserve all history.

### 2. Seller types and auction clocks
- Private seller default confidential bidding 96 hours. At 72 hours prompt to consider lowering minimum (never automatically reduce). Seller may request 24 or 48 hour extension, max one initially, subject to admin approval. Notify invited bidders of revised end; bids retain their own validity and may require renewal.
- Dealer underwrite should prioritise quick answer: selectable urgent 1–2h, today up to 8h, standard 24h; avoid forcing 96h. Dealer may request a price-progress assessment at 48h if an auction is still running, or earlier for urgent deals. Exact timing to be configurable. Dealer can revise their asking price without wiping bids.
- Dealer-facing response: 'On track', 'Close to target', or 'Market below target', with factual caveats and no underlying confidential bid or commercial figures. After internal signoff and confirmed dealer buyer, optionally show a concrete AnyBike underwriting price that could complete the deal now. Avoid guarantees before confirmation.

### 3. Variant-first valuation intelligence
- Mandatory manufacturer + model + exact variant/trim (e.g. GS versus GS Adventure, TE, Rallye, edition) to issue high-confidence valuation; year, registration, mileage, condition, service history, owners, factory options/accessories.
- Canonical manufacturer/model/variant master plus alias mapping for data-source spellings. Never silently merge economically distinct variants. Ambiguous variant => manual review and lower confidence.
- Use authorised/commercially licensed Live Source Hub advertised retail comparable stock, exact variant primary, year/mileage-adjusted secondary, different variant as clearly flagged context only. Display sample count, median, trimmed mean, range, listing age, duplicate detection, time snapshot, confidence and provenance; *asking price is not sold price*.
- Potential external providers to investigate: CAP HPI / Green Book motorcycle valuations, Auto Trader Connect or licensed market/advert feeds, Glass's motorcycle coverage. No assumption of permission for Auto Trader adverts or scraping; obtain licensing, access and pricing before integration.
- Save valuation snapshots per opportunity and reasoned recommended underwriting range; keep internal valuation and dealer-facing progress distinct.

### 4. Formal offer, notifications and audit
- Dealer bid confirmed, seller price latest, HPI/finance ownership checks and provisional condition checks recorded by admin. AnyBike issues its own seller formal offer with expiry and terms; seller account accepts / declines / counters. No auto sale.
- Seller notification on first distinct verified bidding dealer, additional distinct dealer, formal offer and required seller action; updating same dealer bid doesn't create another 'new dealer' notification. Respect notifications/Message Centre and private data.
- All confirmations, author, time, revision and evidence logged.

### 5. Move collection and CHIPS
- Move receives authorised collection booking and declared motorcycle condition/assets to verify. Driver records photos, damage, consumable wear, missing keys, service history etc.
- CHIPS (Collection Handover Inspection & Price Settlement): evidence-based proposed price adjustment, not driver unilateral authority. Seller must agree, AnyBike approve, any dealer bid changes and repair/reconditioning impact reconciled before payment/handover; disagreements escalate or halt collection. Preserve seller dignity, no pressure.
- Potential driver incentive: e.g. starting proposal 10% of completed, documented, seller-approved genuine purchase reduction, subject to independent review, cap and payroll/legal approval. Avoid incentive for invented faults or nondisclosed/misrepresented condition. Track commission separately and reconcile net margin after repair costs, VAT and bid changes.
