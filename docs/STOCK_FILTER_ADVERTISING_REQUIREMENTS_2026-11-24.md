# Available Stock advertising requirements — 24 November 2026

Extend the existing Advertising & Affiliates Mega System and Supabase database; no parallel system or hardcoded creatives.

- Premium finance: one eligible finance campaign (Q-Finance or MotoNovo, subject to commercial agreement and compliance), retained when stock filters change.
- Premium transport: Move Motorcycles retained wherever delivery is relevant and campaign eligibility permits.
- Standard dealer and affiliate campaigns respond to selected manufacturer, model, motorcycle category and applicable country/account rules. A Harley-Davidson cash-purchase advert must not appear for a BMW-only search.
- Limit the total visible adverts by the number of motorcycles on the current results page, allowing premium slots plus a small number of relevant standard placements; exact configurable density remains to be implemented. Never dump all eligible campaigns into the grid.
- Use the existing centrally managed named placements, campaign priority, rotation and tracking. Avoid repeat campaigns and competing finance adverts on a page. Empty standard slots stay empty when nothing matches.
- Re-evaluate on filter and pagination changes, including comparison shortlist handoff. Preserve existing card design, country restrictions, disclosure and consent requirements.
- Admin controls should be simple: premium/standard, category, manufacturer/model/market targeting, priority and density. No manual page editing.

Status: agreed requirements only; advertising logic is not implemented by this document.
