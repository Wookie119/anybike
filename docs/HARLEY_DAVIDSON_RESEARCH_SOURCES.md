# Harley-Davidson model research sources
Updated: 2026-10-13

These are research references for the Harley-Davidson manufacturer page and sixteen model guides. This file is not a licence to reproduce third-party material or images.

## Primary manufacturer references
1. [Harley-Davidson media archive](https://h-dmediakit.com/archive-hub.html) — archived regional model-year releases (2010 onward for US); identify model code, model-year, release dates, trim and generation changes. **Images on this site are restricted to accredited journalistic use. Do not republish them commercially without permission.**
2. [Official Harley-Davidson Service Information Portal](https://serviceinfo.harley-davidson.com/) — model-specific owner manuals, VIN/model codes, service information and technical specifications. Record market and year.
3. [UK Harley-Davidson brochure](https://brochure.harley-davidson.com/en_GB) — year-specific UK specifications and colours. Check publication edition and do not assume the current brochure applies to earlier bikes.
4. [Official Harley-Davidson service resources](https://www.harley-davidson.com/gb/en/content/motorcycle-maintenance/authorized-service.html) — owner manuals, recall and service links.
5. [Harley-Davidson Museum archives](https://www.harley-davidson.com/us/en/experiences/museum/archives.html) — museum-sourced model heritage and historically significant milestones; museum archives are not an open public research database.

## Secondary cross-checks
6. [MotorcycleSpecs.co.za](https://www.motorcyclespecs.co.za/) — historical year-specific specifications for older Road Glide, Street Glide and others. **Cross-check figures against manufacturer manuals and check original measurement method.**
7. [Riding Vintage model reference 1903–1969](https://www.ridingvintage.com/p/model-reference.html) — model codes and early V-twin, Knucklehead, Panhead, Shovelhead and Sportster lineages. **Secondary reference: verify assertions.**

## Editorial standards
- Keep year, market and variant alongside every performance, fuel capacity, seat height, mass, production date, colour and engine specification.
- Distinguish model-year announcement from actual manufacture and UK first registration.
- Use attribution and links; independently paraphrase. Do not copy copyrighted descriptions, charts or images.
- Press images from the H-D Media Site are not cleared for AnyBike commercial display. Use separate properly licensed/model-confirmed photos, preserving author, source and licence.
- Capture source URL and verification status in model records. A secondary reference does not make a specification manufacturer-verified.
- Do not expose Available Stock or alter the live buying, Supabase or login systems.
