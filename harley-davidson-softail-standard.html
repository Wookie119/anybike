Softail Standard: factory identity and custom modifications

The Softail Standard is frequently modified, so a UK listing's appearance alone cannot establish its original specification. A custom handlebar, seat, wheel, exhaust or painted tank may make a different Softail look similar to an FXST.

  • Identify the year: check the VIN, engine number, V5C and original model designation. Early Softail Standard motorcycles and the 2020-onward Milwaukee-Eight-era model must be described separately.
  • Compare original fitment: obtain period manufacturer literature and photographs of the exact year to establish which wheel sizes, bars, seat, lighting and exhaust were factory equipment.
  • Check alterations: inspect cable routing and steering clearance after handlebar changes, wiring after lighting conversions, suspension function and exhaust condition.
  • UK purchase or export: confirm registration records, keys, service documentation, outstanding finance checks and destination-country acceptance of any modifications.

These are due-diligence checks, not a claim that a particular modification is unsafe or that AnyBike approves it.
