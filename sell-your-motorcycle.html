FAQ

Questions about selling your motorcycle.

Do you buy motorcycles nationwide?

Yes. AnyBike reviews motorcycles across the UK and can arrange collection where suitable.

Do you buy from dealers?

Yes. Dealers can offer individual motorcycles, part-exchange stock or multiple bikes.

Do you buy damaged bikes?

Possibly. Please describe the condition honestly and upload photos where possible.

Do I need the registration?

No. Registration helps, but you can still submit make, model, year and mileage manually.

Why does AnyBike buy motorcycles?

AnyBike buys motorcycles for stock, sourcing opportunities and export buyer demand.

How fast will you reply?

We aim to review enquiries quickly. Registration and mileage are enough to start; photos and your target price can speed up the decision.
