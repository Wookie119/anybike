Step 1 · International opportunity

A simpler route to overseas motorcycle buyers

AnyBike helps UK dealerships sell motorcycles to international trade and retail buyers, opening up new sales opportunities without competing with your existing UK business.

Connect your existing stock provider once, and AnyBike can offer suitable used motorcycles, including VAT-qualifying bikes, to international buyers.

When a sale is agreed, AnyBike buys directly from your dealership and handles the overseas customer, UK collection arrangements and export process.

FREE for UK dealers — no listing fees or sales commission.

Step 2 · International markets

Discover the markets AnyBike can reach

AnyBike introduces motorcycles sourced in the UK to buyers across international markets. Different countries have different preferences, buying requirements and import arrangements — from European destinations to markets in Africa, Asia, the Middle East and beyond.

Our country pages help overseas buyers explore UK motorcycle sourcing, delivery options and the buying process. For UK dealers, this means an opportunity to reach buyers beyond your usual UK audience, without having to market motorcycles country by country.

Step 3 · BMW

BMW motorcycles: helping overseas buyers find the right bike

BMW is one example of how AnyBike helps overseas customers discover motorcycles available from UK dealerships. Our growing BMW guides cover model families, different generations, specifications and the details buyers often want to understand before enquiring.

Whether a customer is interested in an adventure bike such as the R 1300 GS, a sports motorcycle such as the S 1000 RR or a touring model, the guides help them narrow down what they want. Where suitable motorcycles have been approved for international publication, buyers can be directed to available stock and can enquire or request a purchase without first creating an account.

What this means for your dealership: connecting your stock gives AnyBike the opportunity to match eligible BMW motorcycles with overseas demand. You do not need to market each bike internationally or deal directly with the overseas customer. When a sale is agreed, AnyBike purchases the motorcycle from your dealership and coordinates the UK-side arrangements.

Step 4 · Harley-Davidson

Harley-Davidson: connecting distinctive motorcycles with overseas buyers

Harley-Davidson attracts buyers looking for a particular riding experience, style or model. AnyBike’s growing guides help international customers explore the differences between touring motorcycles such as the Road King and Street Glide, cruisers such as the Fat Boy and Low Rider S, and newer models including the Sportster S and Pan America.

Customers can compare model generations, specifications and features before deciding what to look for. Where an eligible motorcycle has been approved for international publication, we can connect that interest with available UK dealership stock. Visitors can enquire or request a purchase without registering first.

What this means for your dealership: a Harley-Davidson that is attracting limited interest in your local market may appeal to an overseas customer searching for that specific model or specification. Connecting your stock gives AnyBike the opportunity to identify those buyers without asking your dealership to advertise internationally, handle overseas enquiries or arrange export.

When a sale is agreed, AnyBike buys directly from your dealership and coordinates the agreed UK collection and export arrangements. UK dealers pay no listing fee or sales commission.

Step 5 · UK collection and international shipping

How a motorcycle travels from your showroom to an overseas buyer

Once the purchase is agreed, AnyBike coordinates the UK-side handover and the export arrangements appropriate to the shipment. Your dealership supplies the motorcycle to AnyBike; you do not have to manage international shipping.

1 · Motorcycle securedAnyBike agrees the purchase with the UK dealership and confirms the collection arrangements.
2 · UK collectionMove Motorcycles can arrange collection and transfer to the agreed UK handover point.
3 · UK port and forwarderDepending on the shipment, the motorcycle is delivered to an agreed port or freight forwarder for onward transport.
4 · Overseas shipmentThe freight forwarder and importer coordinate the relevant shipping and destination formalities under the agreed terms.

Examples of UK gateways include Southampton, Felixstowe, Tilbury and London Gateway. The route and paperwork depend on the destination and the buyer's shipping arrangements.
