1. Request a permanent stock feed from your provider

Send a request from your own email account to your software provider. Add their email address and your account number; AnyBike will be copied in to help arrange a test feed and integration.

Select the provider you want to request a feed from.

The email will be addressed to your provider and CC sales@anybike.co.uk. It opens in your own email application; check the From account before sending.

Your provider will receive the request from you, not from AnyBike. Your email application must be configured on this device.

Alternative: CSV / Excel stock upload

Use this only if your provider cannot supply a permanent feed, or to identify VAT Qualifying stock missing from the provider feed.
