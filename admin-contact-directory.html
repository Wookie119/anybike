Marketing permission guidance (read when needed)

Private customers · marketing permission

Some private contacts originated with Move Motorcycles. Their previous marketing opt-out arrangements have not yet been verified. Do not treat an existing contact record as permission to send marketing.

Before preparing a private-customer marketing invitation, check whether consent or every condition of a valid existing-customer soft opt-in can be evidenced. If uncertain, keep the contact on hold for marketing. A different greeting does not change this requirement.

Suggested greeting for eligible private recipients: “Dear Motorcyclist”. Dealership contacts and sole traders may be subject to different marketing rules. Existing permission and suppression records must be respected.

Note: imported @app.movemotorcycles.co.uk addresses are not verified customer inboxes and are excluded from email invitation preparation.
