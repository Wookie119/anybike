Your bottom-line price is private to AnyBike and is never shown to buyers. It does not replace your advertised asking price. It tells AnyBike the confidential minimum price we can work from when a suitable buyer appears. Your bottom-line price is used by AnyBike’s internal AI matching to identify suitable trade buyers and relevant opportunities. This can help clear ageing stock and help you sell a motorcycle before it becomes due or is called off under a stocking plan.

Getting Started with My Dealership

Three simple ways to get value from your AnyBike connection

Your dealership is already connected to AnyBike. You do not need to re-enter motorcycle details or manage the feed here. Use My Dealership to tell us where there is genuine selling flexibility and what motorcycles you want us to find.

1

Your stock arrives automatically

AnyBike receives the motorcycles from your agreed stock system. Your make, model, registration, mileage and asking price remain feed-driven.

2

Add a confidential bottom line

Add one when you own a bike and would trade it, or when a stocking-plan bike is approaching call-off. This gives AnyBike a private floor to work from.

3

Save motorcycles you want to buy

Create Saved Searches for makes and models you need. AnyBike can use those searches to identify matching stock and send suitable opportunities to you.

Why do we ask for a bottom line? Because buyer opportunities can move quickly. If AnyBike already knows the confidential price you can genuinely work from, we can assess buyer demand, identify suitable trade buyers and act before ageing stock becomes harder to move or a stocking-plan motorcycle reaches call-off. The bottom line is not published, not shown to buyers and is controlled by your dealership.
