Generation and identification guide

Dyna and Softail generations

The early Low Rider S used a Dyna chassis. Later generations used the Softail chassis. Equipment, engine and suspension details must be matched to model year.

Low Rider S versus ST

The Low Rider ST has its own factory touring-oriented specification. An S fitted with independent fairings or luggage is not automatically an ST.

Low Rider S: important production generations

Harley-Davidson has reused some model names on very different chassis and engines. These documented milestones distinguish generations; they are not a complete record of every UK specification.

2016 Dyna Low Rider S

The original Low Rider S was a Dyna-platform performance cruiser, not the later Softail Low Rider S.
