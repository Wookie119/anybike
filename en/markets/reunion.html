Frequently asked questions

Buying a UK motorcycle for Réunion.

Can AnyBike find a motorcycle that is not currently listed?

Yes. Send the make, model, year, mileage, colour, budget and equipment you want. AnyBike can look beyond publicly listed stock.

Can I choose my own freight forwarder?

Yes. Choose your carrier or freight forwarder. AnyBike arranges UK collection and handover as agreed.

Can AnyBike arrange overseas delivery?

Yes. Ask AnyBike to arrange collection, crating and shipping to your local port, or delivery in the crate to your home or workplace. You can also use your own freight forwarder.

Can I request photos, videos or an inspection before buying?

Options depend on the motorcycle and seller. AnyBike confirms what is available before you decide.

Which documents come with the motorcycle?

Documents vary by motorcycle. AnyBike confirms what is available before sale, such as the V5C, invoice, service history, keys and other vehicle documents.

Are taxes and registration costs included in the AnyBike price?

Only if expressly agreed. Check import requirements, duties, taxes, compliance and registration in Réunion with the relevant professionals or authorities for the motorcycle you plan to buy.
