# AnyBike catalogue expansion

Approved direction, 27 November 2026: cover every genuine motorcycle model from every manufacturer, including discontinued models and defunct manufacturers. Work backwards from 2026, starting with 2026–2016 across every manufacturer, then 2015–2005, 2004–1994, 1993–1983, 1982–1972, 1971–1961 and successive non-overlapping eleven-year inclusive windows. These are working batches, not production-date assumptions.

Each model requires a canonical identity, category, permanent approved V2 guide, manufacturer-page model pill and card, verified description, manufacturing location and production history where documented, specifications, and a real rights-cleared photograph. Use approved DealerWebs/Pyramid photos where exact identity is confirmed; temporary AnyBike placeholders are not completed photography. Preserve approved manufacturer layouts and existing research/photos. Do not invent missing facts or create duplicate models for feed aliases. Preserve stock and historical advertisements. Never block live stock ingestion for incomplete editorial research or photographs.

Start by reconciling all models produced during 2026–2016 across all manufacturers, including brands no longer trading. Include models whose production overlaps the window, without duplicating models across windows. Prepare researched historical additions privately before publication; do not generate empty public pages or placeholder-heavy manufacturer libraries. Keep live feed ingestion independent of editorial publication readiness. Then move backwards across all brands. Track evidence and gaps separately from published facts. Verify live presentation before declaring a batch complete. No Vercel involvement.

Baseline: previous catalogue audit reported 996 records across 52 manufacturers; this is not a verified historical completeness count. Historical expansion has not yet begun.

## Future marketplace integration

Publish individual model pills alongside each completed model. Preserve stable canonical model IDs and routes for future motorcycle classifieds, motorcycles wanted, parts wanted and parts for sale, including discontinued models. Work in incremental batches with checkpoints suitable for hourly or overnight continuation. Do not publish empty historical pages or block live feeds. Future classifieds functionality is not part of the current implementation.

## Mandatory model hero photography

Every completed motorcycle model page, across all manufacturers and production eras, must have a real, rights-cleared hero photograph of the correct model and generation where identifiable. Preserve approved hero photographs. Use trusted independent editorial sources, Wikimedia Commons with individually verified commercial reuse licences, or manufacturer photographs with explicit commercial reuse permission. Do not use DealerWebs, Pyramid or other dealer-stock photographs for model-page editorial photography. An AnyBike placeholder is a temporary fallback only and never satisfies editorial completion. Do not publish new empty historical pages; do not block live stock ingestion. Track existing missing hero photographs as remediation work.

This editorial photography rule supersedes any earlier suggestion to reuse dealer-feed images on model pages. Dealer stock imagery and feed configuration remain separate and untouched.
