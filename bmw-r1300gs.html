BMW R 1300 GS

The R 1300 GS combines BMW's boxer-engine adventure-bike heritage with a redesigned 1,300 cc engine, road-touring comfort and equipment for mixed-surface journeys.

BMW factory documentation shows R 1300 GS production at Berlin in September 2023. Its public unveiling was 28 September 2023 and it was introduced as a 2024 model. Production start, public presentation and model-year designation are distinct; final production date is unverified.
