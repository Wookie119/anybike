Heritage Classic: important production generations

Harley-Davidson has reused some model names on very different chassis and engines. These documented milestones distinguish generations; they are not a complete record of every UK specification.

Traditional Heritage Softail Classic

Earlier Heritage models combine Softail chassis styling with detachable touring equipment and traditional V-twin engines.

For buyers: Check model code, accessories and original generation.
