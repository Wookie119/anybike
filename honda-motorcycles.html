Honda history and model families

Honda established its motorcycle manufacturing business in post-war Japan and developed a worldwide reputation across road and competition machinery. Its Super Cub helped bring everyday motorcycle transport to a wide audience, while families such as the CB, CBR, Africa Twin and Gold Wing became recognised in different segments.
