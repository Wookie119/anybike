About this motorcycle type

Cruiser motorcycles emphasise relaxed road riding, distinctive styling and low-seat ergonomics. Weight, footpeg position and touring suitability vary widely.

What to consider before choosing

Riding position

Forward foot controls and bar reach may not suit every rider.

Mass and manoeuvring

Check steering lock, weight and storage requirements.

Passenger use

Seat, luggage and suspension options differ greatly.
