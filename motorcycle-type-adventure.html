About this motorcycle type

Adventure motorcycles combine upright riding positions, useful luggage options and the ability to handle varied roads. Some are principally road-tourers; others favour lighter weight and unsealed routes.

What to consider before choosing

Road versus off-road

Wheel sizes, suspension travel and tyre choice influence comfort on tarmac and rough routes.

Weight and seat height

Consider low-speed manoeuvring with luggage, and whether both feet reach the ground comfortably.

Travel capability

Range, luggage mounting, wind protection and passenger comfort matter on longer trips.
