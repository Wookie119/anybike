What happens next?

Tick Interested on each motorcycle you would seriously consider buying, then press Save my choices. Any motorcycle left unticked will be recorded as Not Interested automatically.

For motorcycles you select, AnyBike will contact the seller, confirm availability and current purchase terms. Where possible, we may also ask the seller for a short hold while you consider the personalised offer.

The AnyBike price shown includes the motorcycle, AnyBike's sourcing fee, and the agreed UK collection & delivery to your chosen UK port or approved UK handover point. Overseas freight, import duty, local taxes and costs after the UK handover are not included unless we specifically confirm otherwise.

If the motorcycle is available, we will send you a formal AnyBike offer. If you accept it, we will ask for the required deposit and move quickly to secure the motorcycle.

Storage charges can apply if a motorcycle remains in AnyBike / Move Motorcycles custody because the onward shipping or handover is delayed. If you expect any delay, message us through My Messages so we can discuss the timing, handover plan and any storage implications with you before they become a problem.
