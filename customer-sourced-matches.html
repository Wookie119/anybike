What happens next?

1. Choose the motorcycles you want us to check. Tick Interested on each motorcycle you would like AnyBike to check with the seller. Anything left unticked will be recorded as Not Interested at this time.

If you have a specific question about a motorcycle, add it underneath that motorcycle. AnyBike will ask it as part of the normal seller availability check.

2. AnyBike checks the seller. We will confirm the motorcycle is still available, confirm the current purchase terms and obtain answers to any question you have asked.

3. We send your Formal AnyBike Offer. If everything is satisfactory, your offer will confirm the motorcycle, the AnyBike price, the information received from the seller and your agreed UK delivery / handover point. The offer price includes the motorcycle, AnyBike's sourcing fee, and the agreed UK collection & delivery to your chosen UK port or approved UK freight forwarder / handover point.

4. You decide whether to proceed. If you accept the Formal Offer, you will be asked to pay the minimum deposit shown in the offer. You can pay more than the minimum deposit, or pay the full amount, if you prefer.

5. AnyBike secures the motorcycle. Once your payment has been received and verified, AnyBike will pay the seller as required to secure the motorcycle. We will keep you updated through My AnyBike so you can see what is happening and what we need from you next.

6. Full payment before collection. Any remaining balance must be received in cleared funds before the motorcycle is collected from the seller. This allows AnyBike to complete the purchase and arrange collection without unnecessary delays.

7. Specialist collection and secure custody. Collection is arranged through Move Motorcycles, our specialist motorcycle transport operation. Collection photographs will be added to your portal as soon as the motorcycle has been collected. The motorcycle will then travel to our secure facility on a gated business park with 24-hour CCTV and no external motorcycle-storage signage before onward delivery to your agreed UK port, freight forwarder or handover point.

Storage charges can apply if onward shipping or handover is delayed while the motorcycle remains in AnyBike / Move Motorcycles custody. If you expect a delay, message us through My Messages so we can discuss the timing and any storage implications with you.
