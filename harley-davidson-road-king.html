UK buyer's model-specific checklist

  • Confirm Road King or Road King Special and original trim.
  • Inspect luggage and detachable windshield hardware where fitted.

1994 Road King introduction

The FLHR Road King launched in 1994, bringing classic touring styling to Harley-Davidson's FL touring chassis. Verify FLHR versus FLHRXS Road King Special before comparing trim, wheels and weather protection.
