Buyer FAQ

Buying motorcycles from the UK.

Can I buy one motorcycle?

Yes, if you are purchasing on AnyBike's trade/business sale basis. Browse Available Stock and enquire about the specific motorcycle you want.

Can dealers request multiple motorcycles?

Yes. Bulk buyers should use the buyer request form so AnyBike can match stock and sourcing opportunities.

Do you ship overseas?

AnyBike's normal export journey covers UK collection and delivery to your agreed UK port, freight forwarder or approved handover point. Your freight provider then manages the onward international shipment unless a different arrangement is expressly agreed.

Can I use my own shipper?

Yes. Save your preferred freight forwarder in My AnyBike and confirm which shipping preference you want to use for each purchase. AnyBike reviews the Deal-specific handover choice before delivery.

Does an export motorcycle include a mechanical warranty?

No. AnyBike currently sells used motorcycles only on a genuine trade/business basis. No separate voluntary mechanical warranty, guarantee or promise of future reliability is included unless expressly stated in the Formal Offer. Our collection check is visual/handover only. If you require mechanical assurance, request or arrange an independent inspection before committing to purchase.

How do offers and payments work?

Formal offers, Proformas, payment reporting and purchase progress are handled through My AnyBike. Your dashboard shows what needs your attention and what AnyBike is working on.

Can I arrange an independent inspection?

Yes. If you require additional mechanical assurance, request or arrange an independent inspection before committing to purchase.

Where are the trade sale terms?

Read AnyBike's Trade & Export Sale Policy.
