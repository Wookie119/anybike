Yamaha history and model families

Yamaha entered motorcycle production in the 1950s. The company's motorcycles became closely associated with road racing and off-road competition, alongside widely used road-going models. Its MT naked-bike range, YZF sport bikes, Ténéré adventure models and touring-oriented Tracer machines serve different riders and markets.
