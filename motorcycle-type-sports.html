About this motorcycle type

Sports motorcycles emphasise handling, braking and aerodynamic performance. The category includes road-friendly middleweights and track-focused superbikes.

Road or track

Riding position, heat and suspension setup differ considerably by model.

Running costs

Electronics

Riding modes and assistance systems vary by generation and specification.
