Choosing the right provider

Shipping support for motorcycles leaving the United Kingdom.

Different companies provide different parts of the journey. Some manage international freight from collection to destination, while others specialise in UK collection, packing, container loading or port handover.

What does a freight forwarder do?

A freight forwarder coordinates the movement of cargo through carriers, ports, warehouses and customs processes. For motorcycles, this may include collection, export preparation, booking, packing, documentation, marine insurance and destination-agent arrangements.

The forwarder may not physically carry the motorcycle throughout the journey. It can instead organise services supplied by shipping lines, airlines, hauliers, packers and overseas agents.

Questions to ask before booking

✓
Do you regularly handle motorcycles?
✓
Is the quotation port-to-port or door-to-door?
✓
Are packing, storage and UK collection included?
✓
Who completes the UK export declaration?
✓
What insurance is included or available?
✓
Which destination charges are excluded?

Air freight

Air freight can provide faster transit but normally requires specialist preparation, accurate dimensions and compliance with airline rules.

A specialist UK motorcycle transporter can collect from the seller and deliver to the selected packer, warehouse, depot or port.

Before the motorcycle leaves

Important points to confirm in writing.

Quotation scope

Check whether collection, packing, storage, export clearance, terminal handling, marine insurance and destination delivery are included.

Destination charges

Port fees, customs clearance, duties, taxes, inspections, registration and local delivery may be payable separately.

Motorcycle preparation

Confirm fuel limits, battery rules, keys, alarms, personal items and whether a crate, case or specialist securing method is required.

Import eligibility

The buyer must confirm that the motorcycle can legally be imported, cleared, inspected and registered in the destination country before purchase.
