Customer-facing core wording

VAT-Qualifying motorcycles and UK export VAT

Some motorcycles on AnyBike are identified as VAT Qualifying based on confirmation from the supplying dealer. AnyBike reconfirms the VAT status as part of the purchase process and verifies the seller's VAT invoice before finalising the VAT treatment of the transaction.

Where a motorcycle is exported from Great Britain, the sale may qualify for 0% UK VAT when the relevant HMRC export conditions are satisfied. AnyBike may require VAT or an amount equivalent to VAT to be held until satisfactory proof of export has been received and verified.

Zero-rated UK export VAT does not mean that the motorcycle is free of tax. Import VAT, customs duty, registration charges or other local taxes may be payable in the destination country. The overseas buyer should confirm those costs with their customs or tax adviser.

What proof of export needs to establish

HMRC accepts official or commercial evidence, but the evidence taken together must show that a transaction took place and that the goods actually left the UK.

  • the specific motorcycle, including VIN/registration where available
  • the overseas customer and destination
  • the freight forwarder / carrier
  • the transport/export movement
  • evidence of actual departure from the UK
  • the sale/payment and handover trail
UK freight handover alone is not treated as final proof that the motorcycle has left the UK.
