BMW R 1250 GS · Model reference

The R 1250 GS story

BMW announced the R 1250 GS in September 2018, with European market launches in October. The new 1,254 cc boxer replaced the R 1200 GS engine and introduced BMW ShiftCam variable intake valve timing and lift. The R 1250 GS retained the GS formula of a shaft-driven boxer twin, upright adventure-touring ergonomics and Telelever/Paralever suspension.

BMW quoted 136 PS (100 kW) at 7,750 rpm and 143 Nm at 6,250 rpm. The GS was updated for Euro 5 and revised electronics for the 2021 model period. The later R 1300 GS is a different generation, not simply a renamed R 1250 GS.

History

Introduction and model years

  • 2018 announcement / 2019 model: 1,254 cc ShiftCam boxer introduced; 6-speed gearbox and shaft drive.
  • 2020: Early-generation motorcycles continued; check original equipment rather than registration year alone.
  • 2021: Euro 5 update, standard Dynamic Traction Control, integral ABS Pro and ECO riding mode in BMW's published specification.
  • 2022: Factory equipment changes included an optional aluminium-case holder.
  • 2023: GS Trophy styling introduced in the documented model-year range.

Versions and styles

Standard, Rallye, Triple Black and Trophy

The standard R 1250 GS is distinct from the larger-tank R 1250 GS Adventure. Rallye, Triple Black, GS Trophy and 40 Years GS are factory styles or equipment combinations, not separate engine generations.

UK TE identifies an equipment specification rather than a different engine. Verify Dynamic ESA, heated grips, cruise control, Shift Assistant Pro, keyless ride and luggage fittings against the individual motorcycle's factory build record.

Technical specification

Engine, chassis and dimensions

  • Engine: 1,254 cc air/liquid-cooled boxer twin with ShiftCam; 102.5 × 76 mm bore and stroke.
  • Output: 136 PS / 143 Nm; six-speed transmission and shaft final drive.
  • Suspension: BMW Telelever front and Paralever rear; 190 mm / 200 mm travel in the original factory specification.
  • Brakes: Dual 305 mm front discs; 276 mm rear disc.
  • Wheels: 120/70 R 19 front and 170/60 R 17 rear tyres.
  • Fuel / seat: 20-litre tank and 850/870 mm standard adjustable seat.
  • Weight: 249 kg road-ready in the original published specification; accessories and variants differ.

Paint and editions

Factory colours and special editions

BMW offered several distinctive finishes across the model's life, including the black-and-yellow 40 Years GS anniversary style.

Documented 2023 examples include Light White, Rallye in Light White/Racing Blue/Racing Red, Triple Black in Black Storm Metallic/Black/Achat Gray, and GS Trophy in Gravity Blue Metallic Matte. Availability differed by market and model year.

Check the VIN and factory order details before describing any particular used motorcycle's original colour or trim.

Equipment

Electronics and touring options

Equipment depends on year and options. Early models could have TFT instruments, LED lighting and rider aids, while features such as Riding Modes Pro, Dynamic ESA, Gear Shift Assistant Pro, heated seats, adaptive lighting and tyre-pressure monitoring varied by specification.

The 2021 revision changed the standard electronic equipment. Don't assume two motorcycles with the same registration year or TE badge have identical options.

Buying advice

What to check on a used R 1250 GS

  • Check the service record and outstanding BMW recalls or campaigns by VIN.
  • Confirm the exact model, original equipment and any retrofitted accessories.
  • Inspect suspension, wheels, brakes, tyres and shaft-drive condition.
  • Test the TFT display, switches, electronic suspension and keyless functions where fitted.
  • Check luggage mounts, pannier keys and signs of heavy off-road use.
