How our charges work

AnyBike fee or third-party cost?

Some services are carried out directly by AnyBike. Others are provided by specialist transport, inspection, packing, freight or storage partners. We show these separately wherever possible so you can see what AnyBike is charging and what is being paid to another provider.

AnyBike fee

Our service charge

The amount charged by AnyBike for sourcing, coordination, administration or another agreed service.

Third-party cost

Supplier or partner charge

Transport, packing, freight, storage or other costs charged by an external provider and confirmed as part of the quotation.
