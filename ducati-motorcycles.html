Ducati history and model families

Based in Bologna, Ducati is known for distinctive motorcycle engineering and its involvement in racing. Its road motorcycle families have included the Monster, Multistrada, Panigale, Scrambler and Diavel. They represent notably different riding styles, from upright roadsters and adventure touring to circuit-focused superbikes.
