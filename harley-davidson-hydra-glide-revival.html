1949 Hydra-Glide origins

Hydra-Glide originally described the telescopic hydraulic front fork fitted to Harley-Davidson's E and F motorcycles from 1949. The era's FL touring motorcycles shaped the visual character celebrated by the 2024 Revival.

The historic Hydra-Glide and modern Revival are not mechanically equivalent motorcycles.

2024 Icons Collection Revival

The 2024 limited edition recreated the appearance of a customised 1956 FLH with Redline Red and Birch White paint, vintage-style tank badges, a tall detachable windshield, chrome details and fringed leather-look touring equipment.

Each example was individually numbered. Confirm its serialised identification and original equipment before purchase.

2024 Hydra-Glide Revival: specification and identification

The Hydra-Glide Revival is a 2024 Icons Motorcycle Collection model inspired by the 1956 FLH rather than an original 1950s motorcycle. Harley-Davidson described a limited run of 1,750 individually numbered examples.

  • Engine and transmission: Milwaukee-Eight 114 V-twin (approximately 1,868 cc), six-speed transmission and belt final drive.
  • Running gear: published 2024 specifications list a 49 mm telescopic fork, hidden rear monoshock and 16-inch wire-spoke wheels.
  • Originality: check the serialized riser emblem, Icons Collection fender markings, two-tone windshield, Redline Red and Birch White paint and edition-specific saddle and luggage.
  • Before purchase: confirm service history, original parts and accessories, VIN, ownership records and whether equipment matches the actual UK-market motorcycle.
