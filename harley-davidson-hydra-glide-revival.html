Harley-Davidson Hydra-Glide Revival

A numbered, retro-styled Softail inspired by a custom 1956 FLH, honouring 75 years since Harley-Davidson's original Hydra-Glide telescopic front suspension arrived in 1949.

Worldwide production was limited to no more than 1,750 motorcycles. This is a historic model guide, not a live stock listing.

1949 Hydra-Glide origins

Hydra-Glide originally described the telescopic hydraulic front fork fitted to Harley-Davidson's E and F motorcycles from 1949. The era's FL touring motorcycles shaped the visual character celebrated by the 2024 Revival.

The historic Hydra-Glide and modern Revival are not mechanically equivalent motorcycles.

2024 Icons Collection Revival

The 2024 limited edition recreated the appearance of a customised 1956 FLH with Redline Red and Birch White paint, vintage-style tank badges, a tall detachable windshield, chrome details and fringed leather-look touring equipment.

Each example was individually numbered. Confirm its serialised identification and original equipment before purchase.

UK buyer's checklist

  • Confirm factory FLI model code, 2024 model year and VIN against original paperwork.
  • Check the limited-edition numbered riser badge and Icons Collection details.
  • Inspect unique paintwork, windscreen, seat, saddlebags, trim and chrome for repairs or replacements.
  • Review recorded mileage, service history, recall status, keys, accessories and modifications.
  • Check destination-country import rules before buying for export.
