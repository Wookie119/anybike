BMW R 1300 GS

The R 1300 GS combines BMW's boxer-engine adventure-bike heritage with a redesigned 1,300 cc engine, road-touring comfort and equipment for mixed-surface journeys.

BMW factory documentation shows R 1300 GS production at Berlin in September 2023. Its public unveiling was 28 September 2023 and it was introduced as a 2024 model. Production start, public presentation and model-year designation are distinct; final production date is unverified.

Model-year development timeline

Distinguishing launch dates, model years and equipment changes. Detailed specifications are collected in the technical overview below.

Model-year timeline 2023 announcement to 2026 editions

September 2023: Berlin production and public unveiling of the redesigned R 1300 GS, sold as the 2024 model.

2024 model year: 1,300 cc ShiftCam boxer, redesigned sheet-steel frame, EVO Telelever and EVO Paralever.

2025 model year: Automated Shift Assistant (ASA) became an available option; it was not standard on every motorcycle.

2026 model year: Updated factory colours and editions; colour changes do not necessarily indicate engine or chassis redesign.

Factory & production Berlin manufacturing and launch chronology
