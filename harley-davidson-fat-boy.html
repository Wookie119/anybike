UK buyer's model-specific checklist

  • Distinguish earlier Softail designs from the substantially revised 2018-on platform.
  • Verify original wheels, tyre sizes, exhaust and engine variant.
  • Inspect modifications to suspension, lighting and registration-compliant equipment.
