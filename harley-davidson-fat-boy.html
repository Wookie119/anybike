Manufacturer-documented model information

2018 Softail redesign: The redesigned 2018 Softail platform incorporated a stiffer frame, hidden monoshock and Milwaukee-Eight powertrain, with Fat Boy and Fat Boy 114 listed separately.

This information describes a documented model range or generation; it is not a claim that all motorcycles bearing this model name have the same specification.

UK buyer's model-specific checklist

  • Distinguish earlier Softail designs from the substantially revised 2018-on platform.
  • Verify original wheels, tyre sizes, exhaust and engine variant.
  • Inspect modifications to suspension, lighting and registration-compliant equipment.

These are suggested inspection points rather than a substitute for a qualified mechanical inspection, HPI checks or verifying export documents.

Fat Boy: important production generations

Harley-Davidson has reused some model names on very different chassis and engines. These documented milestones distinguish generations; they are not a complete record of every UK specification.

1990 original Fat Boy

FLSTF introduced with classic solid-disc-style wheels and Evolution-era Softail architecture.

For buyers: Heritage milestone; engine and chassis vary widely across later years.

2018 Fat Boy / Fat Boy 114

Redesigned Softail frame, hidden rear monoshock and Milwaukee-Eight engines; distinct 107/114 variants in the 2018 range.
