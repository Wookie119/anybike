Motorcycle preparation and shipping

Real motorcycles prepared for international transport.

AnyBike coordinates the practical UK work required to move motorcycles safely from sellers, dealers and trade suppliers into the international freight network.

Professional motorcycle export crating

When crating is required, the motorcycle is delivered to an experienced specialist packing company for professional export preparation.

Worldwide shipping coordination

We can work with your existing shipper or help coordinate delivery into a suitable international freight route.

Important: AnyBike does not manufacture export crates in-house. Professional motorcycle crating is carried out by trusted third-party specialist packing companies. AnyBike coordinates collection, delivery, communication and the required handover with the chosen crating or freight provider.
What AnyBike does

Your complete UK motorcycle export partner.

Trade buyers, motorcycle dealers, importers and bulk buyers can use one AnyBike service or ask our team to coordinate the complete UK export process. Motorcycle purchases from AnyBike are supplied on an agreed trade/export basis.

🔍

Motorcycle sourcing

We search UK motorcycles by make, model, year, mileage, condition and budget.

👁️

Viewing and seller checks

We can contact sellers, confirm details and help identify unsuitable or misleading listings.

✅

Visual condition support

Visible condition, identification, mileage, documents, damage, photos and video can be recorded. This is not a mechanical or engineering inspection.

🤝

Purchase coordination

AnyBike can help coordinate communication, agreed terms, collection arrangements and the UK handover.

🚚

Collection anywhere in the UK

Collection can be arranged from private sellers, dealers, auctions, trade suppliers and storage facilities.

📦

Specialist third-party crating

We arrange delivery to trusted professional packing companies when export crating is required.

⚓

UK port delivery

Motorcycles can be delivered to ports, container depots, freight terminals and shipping warehouses.

🌍

Worldwide and door-to-door

We can liaise with shippers and help coordinate worldwide or door-to-door logistics where available.

Trade / export sale basis: AnyBike does not automatically include a separate mechanical warranty with a used motorcycle sold on a trade/export basis. Our collection and standard condition checks are visual/handover checks only. A buyer requiring a mechanical assessment should request an independent third-party inspection before purchase; the inspection cost is payable by the buyer unless expressly agreed otherwise. Motorcycle export FAQ

Questions about exporting motorcycles from the UK.

Can AnyBike source a specific motorcycle?

Yes. Send the make, model, year, mileage, condition and budget required and our team can search the UK motorcycle market.

Can you inspect a bike before purchase?

Viewing, visible condition checks, seller confirmation, photos and video can be arranged depending on the motorcycle and location.

Does AnyBike make export crates?

No. AnyBike does not manufacture crates. Where crating is required, we coordinate delivery to trusted specialist third-party export packing companies.

Can you use my freight forwarder?

Yes. We can contact your nominated shipper, follow their UK delivery instructions and arrange the motorcycle handover.

Can you deliver directly to a UK port?

Yes. Motorcycle delivery can be arranged to ports, terminals, depots and freight warehouses throughout the UK.

Is door-to-door motorcycle shipping available?

Door-to-door service may be available depending on the destination, customs rules and local delivery coverage.
