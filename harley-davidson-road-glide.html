UK buyer's model-specific checklist

  • Confirm whether it is Road Glide, Road Glide Special, Road Glide Ultra or a CVO variant.
  • Check fairing alignment and whether audio, screen and handlebar modifications are original.
  • Inspect hard panniers, latches, touring electrical equipment and evidence of crash damage.

These are suggested inspection points rather than a substitute for a qualified mechanical inspection, HPI checks or verifying export documents.

Detailed model generations and factory references

Different year-and-trim versions can have different engine families, chassis and touring equipment. These two documented reference points help identify the correct motorcycle before requesting UK supply.

2017 Road Glide Ultra (FLTRU)

The factory's 2017 Road Glide Ultra specification records a Twin-Cooled Milwaukee-Eight 107 engine, a frame-mounted sharknose fairing with triple Splitstream vent, dual reflector LED headlamps, linked Brembo ABS and Boom! Box 6.5 GT audio. This is an Ultra model, not the basic Road Glide.

Harley-Davidson manufacturer reference ↗

2024 redesigned Road Glide

Harley-Davidson announced a redesigned Road Glide for model year 2024 with the Milwaukee-Eight 117, revised fairing and infotainment. Do not apply the 2024 engine or display specification to earlier Twin Cam or Milwaukee-Eight 107 examples.
