How the service works

Professional packing arranged through specialist partners.

AnyBike does not manufacture motorcycle crates in-house. We coordinate the transport, communication and handover with experienced third-party export packing providers.

Specialist packing

The motorcycle is delivered to an experienced packing company that can prepare it for the required export transport method.

Collection coordination

AnyBike can arrange collection from the seller, dealership or storage location and delivery to the chosen packing company.

Shipping handover

We can coordinate onward delivery or handover to the freight forwarder, depot, terminal or shipping warehouse.

Important: AnyBike does not manufacture export crates. Crating is carried out by trusted third-party specialist packing companies, with AnyBike coordinating the service and handover.
