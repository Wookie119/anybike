Honda reveals the 2027 Fireblade in Milan

The redesigned range adds an HRC Works Edition alongside the Fireblade and Fireblade SP.

AnyBike Global editorial · 27 November 2026

Three versions of the new Fireblade

Honda unveiled its redesigned CBR1000RR-R Fireblade family at EICMA on 3 November. The 2027 line-up is the standard Fireblade, the Fireblade SP with electronically controlled suspension, and a new HRC Works Edition intended as the basis for World Superbike homologation.

A lighter frame and a revised swingarm

Honda says the new aluminium frame is 440 g lighter than its predecessor. The company also revised the swingarm and how the engine mounts to the frame. Honda is designing the HRC Works Edition to meet road regulations in individual markets. Local availability and road approval depend on the market.
