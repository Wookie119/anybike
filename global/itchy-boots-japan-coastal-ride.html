Itchy Boots takes the coastal route through Japan

Noraly’s solo ride puts Japan’s quieter coastal roads in the spotlight.

AnyBike Global editorial · 27 November 2026

Japan from the saddle

In the 18 November episode of Itchy Boots, Dutch motorcycle traveller Noraly turns the camera towards Japan’s coast. The episode is part of her eighth season. It offers one rider’s view of Japan at road level, with the scenery and the ride sharing the screen.

Follow the ride

The original episode runs for just under 20 minutes. It’s a starting point for readers interested in motorcycle travel and in how a creator tells a story from the saddle. Noraly publishes the episode and the wider series on her own channel.
