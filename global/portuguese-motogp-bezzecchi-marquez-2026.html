Bezzecchi wins in Portugal as Márquez clinches the MotoGP title

Aprilia swept the Portimão podium, while eighth place secured the championship for Ducati’s Marc Márquez.

AnyBike Global editorial · 27 November 2026

Aprilia’s clean sweep

Marco Bezzecchi won the Portuguese Grand Prix at Portimão on 22 November. Aprilia Racing teammate Jorge Martín finished second, with Trackhouse Aprilia rider Raúl Fernández third. It was Bezzecchi’s seventh Grand Prix victory of the season, and it followed his Sprint win on Saturday.

Eighth was enough for Márquez

The Grand Prix win and the championship went to different garages. Márquez finished eighth on his Ducati, enough to settle the premier-class riders’ title with the Valencia finale still to run. Martín’s second place kept him two points ahead of Bezzecchi in the battle for second in the championship.
