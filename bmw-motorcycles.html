BMW Motorrad — history and manufacturing

Discover the manufacturer behind the motorcycles and the engineering that connects its model families.

From aircraft engineering to the R 32

BMW introduced its first motorcycle, the R 32, at the Berlin Motor Show on 28 September 1923. Designed under Max Friz, it established the familiar boxer-engine and shaft-drive identity found throughout BMW's motorcycle history.

Where BMW motorcycles are made

BMW Group Plant Berlin, in Spandau, is a major motorcycle production facility and a centre of BMW Motorrad's modern manufacturing history. Production and assembly arrangements have also evolved internationally; country of assembly depends on the specific model and market.
