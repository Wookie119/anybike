Export eligibility

Export eligibility depends on the motorcycle and destination.

Some motorcycles — particularly new, nearly new or manufacturer-controlled stock — may be subject to supplier, manufacturer, franchise, finance, registration or destination-market restrictions. Export eligibility is confirmed for the intended destination before sale.
