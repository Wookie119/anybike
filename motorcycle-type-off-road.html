About this motorcycle type

Off-road and enduro motorcycles are intended for trails, competition or demanding surfaces. Road legality and maintenance intervals differ from general adventure bikes.

What to consider before choosing

Road legality

Check registration, lights and legal access before planning road or trail use.

Service intervals

Competition-derived bikes can require substantially more frequent maintenance.

Suitability

Tyres, gearing, weight and suspension should match the terrain and rider experience.
