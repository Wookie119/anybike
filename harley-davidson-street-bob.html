Street Bob: important production generations

Harley-Davidson has reused some model names on very different chassis and engines. These documented milestones distinguish generations; they are not a complete record of every UK specification.

Pre-2018 Dyna Street Bob

Earlier Street Bob models use the Dyna chassis and should not be confused with the revised Softail family.

For buyers: Confirm factory model code and suspension layout.
