Street Bob: identify the Dyna or Softail generation

The Street Bob name spans different chassis generations. The earlier FXDB belongs to the Dyna family, while the later FXBB belongs to the redesigned Softail range. It is important to establish the exact year and model code before comparing advertised motorcycles.

  • Identity: match frame VIN, V5C, year and factory model designation; do not assume that parts or specifications transfer between Dyna and Softail versions.
  • Equipment: check handlebar, seat, lighting, exhaust and wheel fitments against the correct production-year reference.
  • Used-bike inspection: check steering movement and cable routing after handlebar changes, suspension operation, tyres, brakes and documented service work.
  • Export suitability: confirm registration documents and whether any modifications affect the requirements of the destination market.
