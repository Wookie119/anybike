Harley-Davidson CVO Pan America

The premium, factory-equipped adventure-touring Pan America from Harley-Davidson Custom Vehicle Operations (CVO). The 2025 CVO Pan America is distinct from the Pan America 1250 Special and the road-focused Pan America 1250 ST.

2025 UK-market specifications

  • 1,252 cc liquid-cooled Revolution Max 1250 V-twin
  • Claimed 150 hp (112 kW) and 128 Nm
  • 299 kg in running order (manufacturer figure)
  • Front 19-inch and rear 17-inch tubeless laced wheels
  • Adaptive Ride Height and semi-active suspension
  • Factory quickshifter, protective equipment and premium luggage

Figures refer to the 2025 UK specification; individual markets and production years may differ.

What buyers should check

  • VIN and exact CVO model designation, not merely CVO accessories
  • Original paint, equipment and fitted luggage
  • Factory recalls, electronics, quickshifter and suspension function
  • Service history, corrosion, crash or off-road damage
  • Number of keys, luggage keys and available documents

Verify each motorcycle with its seller before accepting an offer. This is model information, not a condition guarantee.

CVO Pan America versus other Pan America models

CVO Pan America: a premium-equipped CVO adventure model with its own paint and factory package. Pan America 1250 / Special: the wider adventure family, with different year- and variant-specific equipment. Pan America 1250 ST: a separate road-focused model with 17-inch road wheels. They should not be conflated in listings or photos.
