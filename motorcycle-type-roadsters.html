About this motorcycle type

Naked motorcycles and roadsters offer upright road riding and accessible performance without the full bodywork of a sports bike.

What to consider before choosing

Wind protection

Minimal fairings are enjoyable locally but less sheltered at motorway speeds.

Ergonomics

Handlebar width, seat height and footpeg position determine day-to-day comfort.

Engine character

Compare low-rev flexibility, vibration and throttle response, not just peak output.
